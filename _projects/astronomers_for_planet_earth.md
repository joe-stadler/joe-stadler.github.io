I am part of [Astronomers for Planet Earth](https://a4e.org/) (A4E), an international grassroots movement of astronomy students, educators, amateurs and scientists. It looks at the climate crisis from an astronomical perspective, informs the public about climate science and solutions, and gives astronomers the tools and community to act, including steps towards more sustainable practices in astronomy itself.

Find out more or join: [a4e.org](https://a4e.org/).
